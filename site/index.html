What it does

What you'd want from an AI sidebar, without another subscription.

Reading, summarizing and asking about pages, videos and selections, with whichever model you pick.

Chat with the page

Articles come through Mozilla Readability, so the AI gets the story, not the menus. Flip the Page switch off for a plain chat.

One-click actions

Summarize, key points, explain, ELI5, translate, fact-check, action items. Type / for all of them, or add your own.

Select or right-click

Highlight text for Explain, Summarize, Translate or Ask, answered in place. Right-click pages, links and images for the same.

Any model, any depth

One picker for every provider and model, with thinking depth from Low to Max. Switch between messages.

YouTube, summarized

On a video, ChroMind reads the transcript. Answers cite [mm:ss] moments you can click to jump to.

Several tabs at once

Add other tabs to the chat and compare them side by side. Attach a screenshot when the page is mostly pictures.

Looks like home

The ChroMods look, in mint and cyan.

Dark and light themes, a dot-frost header, and nothing in the way of the page.

Set up in a minute

Bring the AI you already have.

1

Install

One click in Firefox and Zen. In Chrome, Edge and Brave: unzip and Load unpacked.

2

Pick your AI

Signed in to claude.ai? Done. Your server takes its address and password; an API key is one paste.

3

Ask

Alt+Shift+A opens the panel. Alt+Shift+S summarizes the page.
