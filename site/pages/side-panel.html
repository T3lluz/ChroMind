Open it

  • Click the ChroMind toolbar button, or press Alt+Shift+A.
  • Chromium: it's the browser's side panel. Firefox and Zen: it's the sidebar (switch sidebars from its header).
  • The panel follows your active tab. Reopening it brings back the chat you had.

Pick the AI

The pill in the header shows the model and provider answering, with the maker's logo. Click it for the picker: providers on the left, models on the right, depth chips under models that can think harder, and a search box across everything that's ready. See the picker.

The page bar

Under the header, ChroMind shows the tab it's reading. The Page switch decides whether the page goes with your question; ChroMind sends a page once per chat and reuses it for follow-ups. On YouTube it says Video and sends the transcript instead.

  • Add tabs (the tabs icon) puts other tabs in the chat, for comparing or summarizing several pages.
  • Pages ChroMind can't read (browser pages, extension stores, PDFs in the built-in viewer) show ChroMind can't read this page, and you can still chat.
  • Long pages are cut at the limit in Settings → Behavior (60,000 characters by default), and the model is told.

Quick actions and slash commands

An empty chat shows actions for what's in front of you: a page, a video, a selection, a link or several tabs. Type / in the message box for the full list, use the arrow keys and Enter, or keep typing to add instructions: /translate make it formal.

YouTube

On a video page ChroMind opens YouTube's transcript, reads it with timestamps and closes it again. Answers cite moments like [4:12]; click one to jump the video there, in the tab that already plays it. Videos without captions fall back to the page text.

Screenshots and images

The camera button attaches a screenshot of the visible page: handy for charts, designs and image-heavy pages. Right-click any image for Describe image or Ask about this image. Claude, Hermes and vision models on API-key providers can see images, as can OpenCode with a Zen key. Apps through the Bridge can't.

Answers

  • While it works, a small scanner (the one OpenCode uses) shows what's happening: Reading the page, Thinking, Using web_search, Writing, with a timer.
  • Answers stream in as Markdown, with code blocks you can copy and tables that scroll. Each one is labeled with the model and provider that wrote it.
  • Models that think show a collapsible Thinking section; agents like Hermes show the tools they used.
  • Under each answer: Copy, Read aloud, Regenerate, and for Claude, Open in claude.ai.
  • Esc or the stop button ends an answer early.
  • When something's wrong (signed out, a server that doesn't answer), the error says what to do, with a button to the right setting and, where it helps, a command to copy.

History

Chats are saved in the browser (Settings → Data & privacy turns that off). The history button lists them by day with search; Export chat saves the open one as Markdown. The + button starts a new chat.

Full-page chat

Type cm, a space and a question in the address bar. ChroMind opens in a tab and answers there, without page context.
