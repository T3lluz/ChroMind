Browser-wide

KeysDoes
Alt+Shift+AOpen or close ChroMind
Alt+Shift+SSummarize the current page (or video)
Alt+Shift+EExplain the selected text
cm Space question EnterAsk from the address bar (opens a full-page chat)

Change them in Chromium at chrome://extensions/shortcuts, and in Firefox at about:addons → gear → Manage Extension Shortcuts. Settings → Shortcuts has a button for both.

In the panel

KeysDoes
EnterSend (Shift+Enter for a new line; switch to Ctrl+Enter in settings)
/Action menu: ↑ ↓ to pick, Enter or Tab to run
EscStop the answer, close menus and history

On pages

KeysDoes
Shift+arrows to selectShows the selection toolbar, like the mouse does
EscHide the toolbar and close the answer card
