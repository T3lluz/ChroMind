ChroMind sits in your browser's sidebar (Firefox, Zen) or side panel (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Arc). It reads the page you're on and lets you ask about it, summarize it, translate it or explain any part of it, without copying anything anywhere. It's a sibling of ChroMods and shares its look.

Where the answers come from

ChroMind has no server and no account. It talks to the AI you choose, with your own login or your own server:

ClaudeYour claude.ai login in this browser. Zero setup. Claude CodeThe claude CLI and your subscription, through ChroMind Bridge. CursorThe cursor-agent CLI and your Cursor account, through the Bridge. Hermes AgentYour own Hermes server, with its memory and tools.

Anything that speaks the OpenAI chat API (Ollama, LM Studio, OpenRouter, vLLM) works too.

What you can do

  • Ask about the page. The panel shows the tab it's reading. Ask anything; follow-ups keep the context.
  • Quick actions. Summarize, key points, explain, ELI5, translate, simplify, fact-check, action items, suggested questions, and your own.
  • Selection toolbar. Select text for Explain / Summarize / Translate / Ask, answered in a floating card.
  • Right-click menus on pages, selections, links and images.
  • YouTube transcripts with clickable [mm:ss] timestamps.
  • Several tabs in one question, screenshots, chat history with search and Markdown export, read aloud.
  • Address bar: type cm, a space, and your question.
