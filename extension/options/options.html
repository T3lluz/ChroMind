Get started

Your AI, right next to every page.

ChroMind uses the AI accounts you already have. Pick one below, then open the panel with Alt+Shift+A or the toolbar button.

  1. Claude, zero setup. Signed in to claude.ai in this browser? That's it. ChroMind chats through your own login.
  2. Claude Code and Cursor go through ChroMind Bridge, a tiny local helper that runs the claude and cursor-agent CLIs with your logins.
  3. Hermes Agent: enter you@your-server and the SSH password in AI providers (Bridge opens the tunnel).

ChroMind needs access to websites to read pages and reach claude.ai and your servers.

AI providers

The selected one answers by default. Switch any time from the pill in the panel.

ChroMind Bridge

Runs on your computer and lets ChroMind use the claude (Claude Code) and cursor-agent CLIs with the accounts they are signed in to. Nothing leaves your machine except the CLIs' own traffic.

Checking…

Start it (Node 20+), from the ChroMind folder:

node bridge/chromind-bridge.mjs

Start it with your computer:

node bridge/chromind-bridge.mjs install

First time: sign the CLIs in once with claude auth login and cursor-agent login. Bridge guide

Behavior

Quick actions

Your own actions show up in the panel, the / menu, right-click menus and the selection toolbar. Use {{target}} (this page / the selected text / this video), {{translateTo}}, {{title}} and {{url}}.

Shortcuts

Also: type cm then Space in the address bar to ask from there.

Data & privacy

ChroMind has no server. Your questions and page text go only to the provider you pick. Chats and settings stay in this browser.
