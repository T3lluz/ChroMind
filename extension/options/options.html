AI

Pick what answers. The ones marked Your account or Free work right away; switch any time from the picker at the top of the panel.

ChroMind Bridge

Only for Claude Code, Cursor and OpenCode's free models: they run as apps on this computer, and a browser can't start apps. The Bridge is a small helper that does. Start it once; ChroMind finds it and pairs by itself.

  1. Get it (needs Node.js): chromind-bridge.mjs
  2. Start it with your computer, from the folder you saved it in:
    node chromind-bridge.mjs install

Behavior

Quick actions

Your own actions show up in the panel, the / menu, right-click menus and the selection toolbar. {{target}} becomes "this page", "the selected text" or "this video"; {{translateTo}}, {{title}} and {{url}} work too.

Shortcuts

In the address bar, type cm, a space and your question.

Data & privacy

ChroMind has no server. Questions and page text go only to the AI you pick. Chats, keys and passwords stay in this browser.
